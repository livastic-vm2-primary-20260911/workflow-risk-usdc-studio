WHAT THIS DOES NOT DO

No false transactions, tax basis or verified deposits.

A description like “Purchase of 1 SOL” does not prove a real purchase, finalized transaction, chain, ticker identity, date, quote currency, amount paid, fees, ownership or tax classification. Multiple cryptoassets can share a ticker. Decimal strings are preserved exactly rather than converting through floating-point arithmetic. Unmatched records stay visible as rejected, never silently deleted or reclassified. Found a different description pattern?
